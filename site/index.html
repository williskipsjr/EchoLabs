EchoLabs

Stem-aware AI mashups. EchoLabs separates two songs with Demucs, finds tempo, beats and key, then stretches one song's vocals beat by beat onto the other's instrumental and transposes them to a compatible key. Each pair below is played twice: once with EchoLabs and once as a naive overlay with no sync and no transposition.

Try it with your own songs (Colab) Code & benchmarks on GitHub
